Economic Cultures

Three sessions on money for designers who just graduated. Bring your phone: we work with small tools you keep.

  1. Tue
    3 Nov

    What you charge

    Work out what an hour of your work really costs, including the hours nobody pays for, and turn it into a rate you can stand behind.

  2. Thu
    5 Nov

    Who pays

    Write a quote and an invoice, then budget a project of your own: every part, every person, every payer. Practising Fair Practice.

  3. Tue
    10 Nov

    Who with

    The closing session of Blueprints 2026 brings it all together. Team up, share costs and take on work none of you could do alone. This is where more resilient economic cultures start.
