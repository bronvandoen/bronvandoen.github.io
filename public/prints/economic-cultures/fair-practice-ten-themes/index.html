Ten questions to ask yourself

The Dutch edition of the Fair Practice Code (2024) adds ten themes, each with a question. The English edition from 2019 doesn’t have them yet, so here they are, in our translation. Each theme turns one of the code’s values into a question about everyday work. Read them as the designer, and again as the one who commissions.

Professional development

How do you contribute to the development and growth of yourself and of the people you work with?

Healthy work

Is the workplace somewhere everyone can work in a healthy and sustainable way?

Safe work

Does everyone feel socially safe in the workplace?

Copyright

Do you always pay the rights holder when you use someone else’s work? Do you make sure your own work is protected, and that you get credit for it?

Diversity & inclusion

How can you make your organisation accessible to diverse talent?

Sustainability

How do we make sure that everyone who works in the sector can build a sustainable career, and keep it?

Transparency in finances

Do you give others insight into your finances? Is the financial accounting transparent to everyone? Do you know who makes the decisions?

Oversight

How is oversight ensured within the institution?

Working on fair practice

Does fair practice get enough time and attention within the organisation? As a freelancer, do you ever ask for feedback yourself, or agree on moments to evaluate together?

Fair pay

Do you get paid fairly? Do the people you commission or employ get a fair fee for their work?

TALK

“How am I expected to practise care when I don’t feel cared for and respected?”

Where, in your own work, does the code stay a nice idea and not yet a practice?
