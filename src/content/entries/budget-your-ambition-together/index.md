A thirty-minute edition of [Our project budget](/entries/our-project-budget/), made for one afternoon in Rotterdam. Each team starts from a situation: a place, a starter statement to make their own, the parts of the work, and two backers already in the budget. The team gives every part to someone, finds a third partner, moves every line to a backer until the money balances, and says why the project deserves fair support.

The five situations, each opening the tool on its own board:

1. [What the harbour remembers](/tools/economic-cultures/expanding-practices/?card=1)
2. [The empty shop](/tools/economic-cultures/expanding-practices/?card=2)
3. [300 stalls later](/tools/economic-cultures/expanding-practices/?card=3)
4. [The next hot summer](/tools/economic-cultures/expanding-practices/?card=4)
5. [Work that counts](/tools/economic-cultures/expanding-practices/?card=5)

The situations are hypotheticals, inspired by real ones. Fonds Lef is an imaginary national fund for the creative industries; its rules follow the Stimuleringsfonds' good practice, without being bound by its terms.
