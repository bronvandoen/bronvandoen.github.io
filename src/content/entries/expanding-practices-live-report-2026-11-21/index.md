On 21 November we hosted two rounds of [Budget your ambition, together](/entries/budget-your-ambition-together/) at Expanding Practices, Nieuwe Instituut, Rotterdam. Teams of people who had mostly just met picked a situation drawn from Rotterdam and wrote a budget of about €55,000. At the end of each round, every team sent its plan to this page, and we looked at them together on the screen.

The page is now the record of that afternoon: four teams, nineteen people, two rounds. Open a team to see who does the work and who pays for it, and what is still open. The names and the claims are the teams' own, as they typed them.

Fonds Lef is an imaginary fund; its rules follow the Stimuleringsfonds' Regeling Vormgeving. To write a budget of your own, [open the tool](/tools/economic-cultures/expanding-practices/).
